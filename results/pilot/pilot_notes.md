## Timestamp granularity (§3.2)
Of 55,028 Track A timestamps, 99.77% are exactly `00:00:00 UTC`. The
generated report says "intraday" because `timestamp_granularity` flags any
non-midnight value; the data are effectively **date-only**, so the §3.2
date-only convention applies. Ingestion keeps the stamped date (drops the
UTC label without converting) so no article moves to an earlier day.
